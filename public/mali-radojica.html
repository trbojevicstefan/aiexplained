Mali Radojica

Мали Радојица

A young hajduk from the folk poem in Vuk Karadžić’s collection. Captured, he played dead. His captors laid fire on his chest, put a snake in his shirt and drove nails under his fingernails, and he did not stir. He smiled only when the girl Hajkuna led the kolo, and she hid his face under her silk scarf.
